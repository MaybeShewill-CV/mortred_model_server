per-model cost: `scripts/new_model.py --check` proves every template renders for
all 10 tasks x 3 backends, refuses to overwrite without `--force`, and
`rtdetr_detector_output_contract_unittest` proves the rendered C++ compiles and
instantiates. Adding a new model now starts from a compilable skeleton instead
of a copied 200-line `.inl`.
