## Phase 7 sam predictor delta

SamPredictor migrates to the multi-session base; SamAutoMaskGenerator is
deliberately kept on BackendCvModel. The deterministic digest changed to
`c065f53137d2642d77fa746c84573a7c37c67999026d29de89010d7a19d9efe3`.

| Metric | lightglue | sam predictor |
|---|---:|---:|
| `model_implementation_lines` | 15371 | 15330 |

`sam_predictor.inl` drops 67 net lines: the four separate double-reset blocks
are replaced by init_sessions(), and the two SAM components now take a
non-owning session pointer instead of owning it.

### Why SamAutoMaskGenerator stays

Its decoder is not a second engine but a pool of N identical sessions (one per
parallel executor, default 4) recycled through a BlockingConcurrentQueue and
driven by Workflow parallel series, with its own WORKFLOW_library_init call.
That is a different abstraction from a multi-engine lifecycle; forcing it into
MultiSessionModel would mean building a second base class for exactly one
model.
